Inertia extensions of Krattenthaler's identity for Sun's Hankel determinant conjecture

Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

Published paper · Version 2 · CC BY 4.0

PDFSourceCitationRepositoryMathesis recordDOILean source

Abstract

For Sun's On some determinants with Legendre symbol entries, arXiv:1308.2900v5, Conjecture 3.16, p. 17, we extend the zero-class identity credited to C. Krattenthaler in arXiv:1308.2900v6 (April 24, 2014), Remark 3.16. For A006077, the Hankel determinant of order n+1 vanishes exactly when n is congruent to 1 modulo 3; otherwise its quotient by (-1)^floor((n+1)/3) 6^n is a positive odd integer. Binomial translation gives a three-block factorization. Integral row normalization reduces parity to the Rueppel determinants, and a positive moment representation gives the sign. We also give parameter-independent and square-class consequences, the exact inertia, and a two-parameter deformation. Related moment arguments establish the harmonic sign assertion in Conjecture 3.9(i) and the two squared-number assertions in Conjecture 3.17.
